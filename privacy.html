Privacy policy

gws-ejung is a private Google Workspace connector operated by Eric Jung. Effective October 9, 2026.

Google data and its use

Depending on the permissions granted to each connected account, the connector can access email content and metadata, calendar information, contacts, and permitted Drive files, documents, and spreadsheets. The assistant account also has previously granted Gmail-management, Gmail-settings, and calendar-editing permissions.

Google data is used to perform the owner's requested tasks and authorized background assistant checks. The connector does not offer access to other users and does not sell Google data or use it for advertising.

Processing, storage, and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's controlled connector environment. Selected Google data may be processed by the configured AI services used by the personal assistant to carry out authorized tasks. Those services' handling of submitted information depends on their terms and the owner's settings.

Requested content and task results may be retained in private assistant conversations, native memory, or automation output. They remain until removed through the owner's storage and conversation controls. This policy does not promise a fixed automatic deletion period.

The public information pages do not connect Google accounts, collect Google credentials, or display private Google data. They contain no analytics scripts or account sign-in forms.

Control and contact

The owner can revoke the connector's Google access using Google Account third-party connection controls and remove locally retained information through the private assistant environment. Revoking access does not itself remove previously retained local information.

Questions and requests should go to the support email displayed on the Google consent screen.
