gws-ejung

A private Google Workspace connector operated by Eric Jung for his personal assistant. This is not a public service and does not offer account registration.

The connector supports owner-authorized email and calendar tasks and access to permitted files, documents, spreadsheets, and contacts. Access is limited to the permissions granted for each Google account.

The personal account uses read-only Google permissions. The separate assistant account has existing Gmail-management and calendar-editing permissions; actions such as sending email still require an explicit owner task.

For questions, use the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
